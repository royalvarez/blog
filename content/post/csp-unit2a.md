## Overview
  IN this section, we will learn the origin story of the internet, and the bedrocks that compose it.

## ARPANET
  ARPANET ((advance research project agency network)) was a secret defense project that began out of fear. Its problem was to solve communication across the nation during a nuclear attack, persisting through the aftermath.

  The solution was packet switching. Packets are data formed by breaking down files into smaller pieces. These packets would then be transfered by switches, routing to its destination and reassembling the data back into the initial file.

## What is the Internet
  The internet in its most simplest form is computational devices communicating information across the globe.

  It is an open network which means that any device can join as long as the device follows specific protocols.

  Protocols define how the devices will interact with one another.

## Blocks of the Internet
  There are 5 main components:
1. Wires and wireless

  The physical hardware of the internet connecting devices and the conversion of electromagnetic signals to binary.

2. IP

  A protocol that deligates IP addresses to devices and gives a routing method to send information to a destination IP address.

3. TCP

  A protocol which transfers packets from one device to another, checking for errors during movement.

4. TLS

  A protocol that encrypts packets so that malicious actors cannot access sensitive information.

5. Http and DNS

  A protocol for dealing with the world wide web and a system for labeling IP addresses to more friendlier human readable names.
