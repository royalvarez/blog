## Overview
In this section, we will understand how creative works  get handled on the world wide web, what kind of rights are given to the author, laws passed to enforce copyright, and the role of licenses for both creative work and code.

## Legal Implication of CopyRight
The Berne convention states that once a creative work is in a preceived medium, copyright is assigned to it. The only way that the copyright is valid in court is  if the author decides to formally register the creative work.

  The heart of copyright lies with the [idea–expression dichotomy](https://medium.com/intellectual-property-laws/idea-expression-dichotomy-in-copyright-law-2bd15576ec17):
  
* "expressions such as words, images, and scenes," are copyrightable.
* But "ideas such as themes, methods, and facts," are unable to be marked as copyright infringement.

  Authors' rights include:
* right to produce their work.
* Right to creative parodies of their work
* Right to sell their work.
* Right to visually display their work
* right to perform their work in various mediums.

  Copyright, however, can expire and enter the public domain. In the public domain, the creative works can be used by anyone for any purpose.

### Doctrine of Fair Use
The doctrine of fair use allows for copyrighted works to be used in some limited matter.
  IN determining what is or is not fair use, there is a list of factors to be considered by the courts. Here are 4:
1. What is the character and purpose of the use? Is it transitive or iterative, is it for education or entertainment.
2. What is the nature of the creative work? Fact verse fiction.
3. How much of the copyright work was used?
4. What kind of impact is made by using the copyrighted work?

### Digital Rights Management (DRM)
The devil's rich market, I mean, the digital rights management are tools designed to restrict access of creative works.

  A popular example of DRM is an audiobook from Audible. You cannot legally download any of the books, and the only way to access a book is from their library on either the website or application.
If you were to download a book, the data would be encrypted. However, there are tools which serves to decrypt drm restrictions, but that is out of the scope of this post.

### Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)
As previously noted, there are tools which only exist to remove DRM. IN response, the Digital Millenium Copyright Act was created to outlaw the creation and use of the tools.
  Nowadays, the DMCA is used to take care of many copyright infringements. If you group up in the 2010s, Youtubers and copyright claim wars were all the rage.

## Licensing Creative Works
Authors can apply licenses to their creative work which describes how distribution will be handled. This makes it so some of the copyright restrictions are uplifted so that people can utilize the creative work more freely.

### Creative Commons
Creative commons is by far the most popular for licensing creative works. It is an organization that distributes six licenses.

  Each license requires attribution to the author.
In where they differ is with these conditions:
* Share alike (sa)
  The work must have the same CC license as the original work.
* Non-commercial (nc)
  The work cannot be used for commercial purposes.
* No derivatives (nd)
  The work cannot be modified.

### Open Source
Like creative commons, open source has its own licenses for the reusability of code. The most common is the [Mit license](https://opensource.org/license/mit). Another kind of license is the copyleft. It is essentially the share alike condition from CC licenses.

  The benefit to open source is that programmers need not to rewrite the same code across companies as it is on some remote service.

  The drawback is that anyone can see the code, even malicious actors. They can find exploits and use them across code bases.
  Another negative is that someone must maintain the code base. They will need to fix bugs and answer any community feedback.

## Summary
Copyright protects authors creative work with the help of the fair use doctrine. Another way is with the digital rights managment which gave rise to the DMCA.
Authors can open their work to the public by either ,way of licenses or putting the work into the public domain. Similarly, programmers can use open source licenses on their code bases.
